    FAQ

    Good questions

    I've never tried aerial yoga. Can I join?

    Yes. Classes are for all levels and the hammock takes your weight from the first session. Age 12+. Not suitable during pregnancy or with untreated blood pressure or eye pressure conditions.

    Which language is the class in?

    Adult classes run in English, Russian and Ukrainian, often mixed. Kids cheerleading is taught in Russian and Ukrainian.

    What if it rains on Cliffside Sculpt?

    The coach confirms the session on WhatsApp on Saturday morning. In bad weather it moves into the studio.

    How do I cancel?

    In the booking app, up to 2 hours before class. The credit goes back to your pass.

    Is there parking nearby?

    The studio is in central Cascais, a short walk from the train station. Paid street parking and public car parks are close by.
