    FAQ

    I have never tried aerial yoga. Can I join?

    Yes. Classes are for all levels and the hammock takes your weight from the start. Age 12+. Not suitable during pregnancy, with untreated high or low blood pressure or eye pressure conditions.

    Which language is the class in?

    Adult classes are taught in English, Russian and Ukrainian, often mixed. Kids cheerleading runs in Russian and Ukrainian.

    What happens if it rains on Cliffside Sculpt?

    The coach confirms the session on WhatsApp on Saturday morning. If the weather is bad, it moves into the studio.

    How do I cancel?

    In the booking app, up to 2 hours before class. The credit returns to your pass.

    Is there parking nearby?

    The studio is in the centre of Cascais, a short walk from the train station. Paid street parking and public car parks are nearby.
