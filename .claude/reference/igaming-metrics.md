## Acquisition

- **Cost per Click (CPC)** — Spend / Clicks. Cost of traffic. Price of curiosity. Comparing ad platforms.
- **Cost per Mille (CPM)** — (Spend / Impressions)×1000. Cost of visibility. Paying for eyeballs. Awareness campaigns.
- **Cost per Install (CPI)** — Spend / Installs. App efficiency. Cost of "just trying it". Mobile UA.
- **Cost per Registration (CPR)** — Spend / Registrations. Funnel efficiency. Cost of commitment. Landing tests.
- **Cost per First Deposit (CPFD)** — Spend / FTD. Real acquisition cost. Price of trust. ROI control.
- **First Time Depositors (FTD)** — Count. New paying users. Fresh blood. Daily KPI. *Affiliate fraud inflated signups; removed 3 partners → CPA normalized.*
- **Registration Conversion Rate** — Registrations/Visits×100. Landing quality. Who didn't bounce. CRO.
- **FTD Conversion Rate** — FTD/Registrations×100. Monetization strength. From curious to serious. Onboarding review. *Registrations up, FTD flat; traffic GEO reallocation improved conversion.*
- **Deposit Conversion Rate** — Depositors/Active×100. Revenue activation. Wallet unlock rate. Funnel audit.
- **Blended CPA** — Total Spend/FTD. Overall efficiency. Average cost of hope. Board reporting.
- **Conversion Lag** — Avg Reg→FTD Time. Decision speed. Thinking time before risk. CRM timing.
- **Click-to-Reg Rate** — Registrations/Clicks×100. Ad quality. Click wasn't wasted. Creative test.
- **Traffic Quality Score** — CR×LTV. Filters junk. Separates players from bots. Affiliate review.
- **GEO Activation Cost** — Spend GEO/FTD GEO. Market entry benchmark. Price of new territory. GEO expansion.
- **Paid vs Organic Ratio** — Paid FTD/Organic FTD. Channel dependency. Addiction to paid traffic. Growth strategy.
- **Organic Traffic Share** — Organic/Total×100. Brand pull. Free traffic power. SEO.
- **Install-to-FTD Rate** — FTD/Installs×100. Full funnel health. From download to deposit. UA review. *Campaign attracted demo traffic; shift to deposit intent → +15% FTD.*

## Revenue

- **Gross Gaming Revenue (GGR)** — Bets−Wins. Core margin. The real scoreboard. Game analysis. *GEO mix shift revealed LATAM growth opportunity.*
- **Net Gaming Revenue (NGR)** — GGR−Costs. Net profit. After reality hits. Partner payouts. *Bonus overspend reduced NGR; promo rules tightened.*
- **ARPU** — Revenue/Users. User value. Average wallet weight. GEO compare. *Germany ARPU 2x Spain; budget reallocated.*
- **ARPPU** — Revenue/Paying Users. Paying user value. Real players only. VIP segmentation. *Many micro depositors; VIP ladder introduced.*
- **Revenue per FTD** — Revenue/FTD. Campaign ROI. How good was the hunt. Media buying.
- **Average Bet Size** — Bets/Count. Risk appetite. Size of courage. Slot math tuning. *Tier-1 users betting small; added bet presets → +18% ARPU.*
- **Hold Rate** — GGR/Bets×100. Margin %. House advantage level. RTP check.
- **Real RTP** — Wins/Bets×100. Actual payout. Luck reality. Compliance. *New slot at 97% RTP under-monetized; reduced to 95.5% → margin improved.*
- **Bonus Cost Ratio** — Bonuses/GGR×100. Promo sustainability. Promo addiction level. Promo planning. *Bonus ratio exceeded 28%; tightened wagering rules.*
- **Deposit Growth Rate** — (Cur−Prev)/Prev×100. Revenue trend. Wallet inflation. Forecasting.
- **High Roller Contribution** — HR Rev/Total×100. VIP dependency. Big fish weight. Risk planning. *One whale churned → -9% GGR; VIP manager assigned.*
- **Revenue Concentration** — Top10%/Total×100. Revenue spread. Eggs in baskets. Segmentation. *82% dependency risky; expanded catalog to reduce exposure.*
- **Cohort ARPU** — Rev Cohort/Users Cohort. Segment value. Which group pays. Cohort review.
- **Cohort LTV** — ARPU×Lifetime. Long-term value. Lifetime wallet. CPA decisions.
- **Margin per GEO** — NGR GEO/Rev GEO×100. Regional strength. Country profitability. Market focus.
- **Avg Deposits per User** — Deposits/Users. Spend intensity. Wallet rhythm. Monetization.
- **Deposit Size Growth** — (Cur−Prev)/Prev×100. Wallet expansion. Bigger bets trend. VIP review.
- **Active Depositors** — Count. Revenue core base. Paying crowd. Revenue report.
- **Bets Sum** — Total wagered. Drives GGR. Money engine. *Revenue dropped 12%; GEO analysis showed PSP issue in Brazil; fix restored volume in 3 days.*
- **Deposit Count** — Number of deposits. Funding flow. ATM visits. *Deposit count stable but size rising → healthy sign.*
- **Avg Deposit** — Average deposit size. User value. Wallet depth. *PSP fee discouraged large deposits; negotiated lower rate.*
