## Description
**Description:**
Run an A/B experiment to evaluate the impact of different landing page hero section designs on signup conversion rate and engagement.
The goal is to identify which hero section concept performs best by testing five different variants with different visual approaches

Design assets:[https://www.figma.com/design/SR3CJfYhLtIHHP3b1E2wbA/VB-Website-Assets?node-id=1-2&p=f](https://www.figma.com/design/SR3CJfYhLtIHHP3b1E2wbA/VB-Website-Assets?node-id=1-2&p=f)

Different hero section designs may significantly impact first impression, perceived product relevance, and user engagement, leading to improvements in Signup Conversion Rate (CR%).
