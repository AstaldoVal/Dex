## Description
**Description:**


Enable SMS as a marketing communication channel by integrating Customer.io with OneSignal's SMS capabilities.
The implementation should include:
• Configure SMS messaging in OneSignal and prepare it for production use.
• Integrate OneSignal with Customer.io so SMS messages can be triggered through Customer.io campaigns and journeys.
• Ensure user phone numbers can be synced and used for SMS messaging.
• Verify that SMS messages can be successfully triggered from Customer.io and delivered through OneSignal.
• Configure SMS subscription status and user mapping between Customer.io and OneSignal.
• Investigate SMS marketing compliance requirements for target markets (primarily the US), including:
    ◦ Whether additional user consent or opt-in flows are required.
    ◦ Carrier and regulatory requirements (e.g., A2P 10DLC registration or equivalent requirements used by OneSignal's SMS infrastructure).
    ◦ Opt-out requirements (STOP, HELP, etc.).
    ◦ Risks of message filtering, throttling, or carrier blocking.
• Document findings, recommendations, required legal/compliance updates, and any implementation gaps before SMS campaigns can be launched.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
  • OneSignal SMS is configured and ready for production use.
  • Customer.io is integrated with OneSignal for SMS messaging.

  • SMS compliance investigation is completed and documented.
  • Any required user consent, opt-in, or legal updates are identified and documented.
  • Requirements to avoid carrier filtering or message blocking are documented.
  • Recommendations and next steps are shared with the team.
